## Required content or factual anchors

- A bar chart compares magnitudes across categories with separated bars.
- A line graph commonly shows change across an ordered sequence such as time.
- Each daily-life example must match the information structure and identify axes or categories.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not imply that a line between unrelated categories proves continuity or causation.
- Do not invent local addresses, prices, schedules, rules, or named evidence not supplied by the prompt.
