Тікелей демократия мен өкілді демократияны жалпы қағидалары бойынша салыстырыңыз. Азаматтардың шешім қабылдауға қатысу жолдарын және әр тәсілдің бір шектеуін атаңыз.

## Required content or factual anchors

- Direct democracy lets eligible citizens decide specified questions themselves.
- Representative democracy delegates routine lawmaking or governance to elected representatives.
- A fair comparison must name participation mechanisms and one practical limitation of each.

## Acceptable variants and exemptions

- Examples and wording may differ if the same factual and causal relationships remain explicit.
- A concise answer may omit secondary detail, but it must preserve every prompt requirement and avoid false certainty.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not claim either form eliminates institutions, rules, minority rights, or implementation constraints.
- Do not invent quotations, named studies, laws, measurements, or local facts that the prompt does not supply.
