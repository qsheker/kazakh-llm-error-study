## Prompt

Қоғамдық Wi-Fi желісін пайдаланушыларға қауіпсіз мінез-құлық жөнінде ақпараттық мәтін жазыңыз. Желінің атауын тексеру, құпия операцияларды шектеу және құрылғыдағы ортақ қолжетімділікті өшіру туралы айтыңыз.

## Required content or factual anchors

- Confirm the exact network name and login process with the venue before connecting.
- Avoid banking, payment, identity, and other sensitive transactions; prefer a trusted mobile connection.
- Disable file sharing and automatic connection, keep software current, and forget the network after use.

## Acceptable variants and exemptions

- The guidance may be localized only after checking the responsible authority or operator's current official channel.
- A neutral template may use clearly marked placeholders where the prompt intentionally withholds local details.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not claim HTTPS or a VPN makes an untrusted device or fraudulent site safe in every respect.
- Do not invent addresses, times, fees, deadlines, eligibility rules, contact details, or requests for unnecessary personal data.

## Sources

- CISA: Cybersecurity While Traveling. https://niccs.cisa.gov/sites/default/files/documents/pdf/ncsam_cybersecuritywhiletraveling_508.pdf Accessed 2026-11-15. Supports: network-name verification and limits on sensitive activity over public Wi-Fi.
