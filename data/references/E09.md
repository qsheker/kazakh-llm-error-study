## Prompt

Пайыздық өсім мен пайыздық тармақ айырмасын салыстырыңыз. Екі көрсеткішке де бір сандық мысал келтіріп, оларды шатастыру қандай қате қорытындыға әкелетінін айтыңыз.

## Required content or factual anchors

- A percentage-point difference subtracts two percentages directly.
- A percentage change divides the change by the starting value.
- For a rise from 20% to 25%, the difference is 5 percentage points and the relative increase is 25%.

## Acceptable variants and exemptions

- Examples and wording may differ if the same factual and causal relationships remain explicit.
- A concise answer may omit secondary detail, but it must preserve every prompt requirement and avoid false certainty.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not label a five-percentage-point increase as a five-percent increase in that example.
- Do not invent quotations, named studies, laws, measurements, or local facts that the prompt does not supply.
