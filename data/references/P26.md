## Prompt

Интернет-дүкеннен тауар алуды жоспарлаған адамға тексеру чек-листін жазыңыз. Сатушы деректерін, толық бағаны, жеткізу және қайтару шарттарын алдын ала қарауды қосыңыз.

## Required content or factual anchors

- Verify the seller identity and contact information across the site and independent sources.
- Check the complete price, currency, recurring charges, delivery terms, return conditions, warranty, and privacy terms before paying.
- Save the product description, order confirmation, receipt, and seller promises.

## Acceptable variants and exemptions

- The guidance may be localized only after checking the responsible authority or operator's current official channel.
- A neutral template may use clearly marked placeholders where the prompt intentionally withholds local details.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not treat reviews, a padlock icon, or a low price alone as proof of legitimacy.
- Do not invent addresses, times, fees, deadlines, eligibility rules, contact details, or requests for unnecessary personal data.

## Sources

- US FTC: Online Shopping. https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/online-shopping Accessed 2026-11-15. Supports: seller checks, full cost, delivery, returns, records, and dispute escalation.
