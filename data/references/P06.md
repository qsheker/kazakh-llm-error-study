## Prompt

Ғимараттағы су беру уақытша тоқтатылатыны туралы бейтарап хабарландыру үлгісін жазыңыз. Нақты уақыт пен мекенжайды ойдан қоспай, тұрғындар қай ақпаратты ресми көзден тексеруі керегін көрсетіңіз.

## Required content or factual anchors

- Use placeholders for affected address or area, start time, expected restoration, and responsible organization.
- State only the confirmed reason, if the official source provides one.
- Direct residents to the official update channel and include emergency guidance only when the authority supplies it.

## Acceptable variants and exemptions

- The guidance may be localized only after checking the responsible authority or operator's current official channel.
- A neutral template may use clearly marked placeholders where the prompt intentionally withholds local details.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not fabricate times, addresses, cause, compensation, or safety instructions.
- Do not invent addresses, times, fees, deadlines, eligibility rules, contact details, or requests for unnecessary personal data.
