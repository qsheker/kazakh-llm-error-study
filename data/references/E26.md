## Prompt

Құрылғыда көптеген бағдарлама қатар ашылғанда оның баяулауының ықтимал себептерін түсіндіріңіз. Жедел жад, процессор жүктемесі және фондық үдерістер арасындағы байланысты көрсетіңіз.

## Required content or factual anchors

- Open applications and background processes compete for finite memory, CPU time, storage I/O, and other resources.
- Memory pressure can trigger compression or swapping, which is slower than direct RAM access.
- A diagnostic explanation should present these as likely causes, not a guaranteed diagnosis.

## Acceptable variants and exemptions

- Examples and wording may differ if the same factual and causal relationships remain explicit.
- A concise answer may omit secondary detail, but it must preserve every prompt requirement and avoid false certainty.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not claim that RAM and storage are the same or that closing any one program always solves the problem.
- Do not invent quotations, named studies, laws, measurements, or local facts that the prompt does not supply.
