## Prompt

Ауа қысымының биіктік артқан сайын неге өзгеретінін түсіндіріңіз. Себеп пен салдарды байланыстырып, күнделікті өмірде байқалатын бір мысал келтіріңіз.

## Required content or factual anchors

- Air pressure is the force produced by moving air molecules colliding with surfaces.
- Higher altitude leaves less overlying air and lower molecular density, so pressure usually decreases.
- A daily-life example may use ears, sealed packages, or boiling, but it must preserve the cause-and-effect chain.

## Acceptable variants and exemptions

- Examples and wording may differ if the same factual and causal relationships remain explicit.
- A concise answer may omit secondary detail, but it must preserve every prompt requirement and avoid false certainty.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not claim pressure is zero on mountains or that altitude alone fixes an exact pressure regardless of weather.
- Do not invent quotations, named studies, laws, measurements, or local facts that the prompt does not supply.
