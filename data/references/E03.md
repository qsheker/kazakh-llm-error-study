## Prompt

Жылу өткізгіш пен жылу оқшаулағыш материалдарды салыстырыңыз. Әр түрге екі мысал келтіріп, ас үйдегі немесе үйдегі қолданылуын түсіндіріңіз.

## Required content or factual anchors

- Conductors transfer thermal energy readily; metals are common examples.
- Insulators slow heat transfer; wood, air, foam, and many plastics can serve as examples.
- The application must link material choice to the desired heat-transfer rate, not merely list objects.

## Acceptable variants and exemptions

- Examples and wording may differ if the same factual and causal relationships remain explicit.
- A concise answer may omit secondary detail, but it must preserve every prompt requirement and avoid false certainty.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not say an insulator prevents all heat transfer or that all metals behave identically.
- Do not invent quotations, named studies, laws, measurements, or local facts that the prompt does not supply.
