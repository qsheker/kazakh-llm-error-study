## Prompt

Файлды қоқыс жәшігіне жіберу оны бірден және толық жояды деген пікірді түзетіңіз. Қоқыс жәшігі, сақтық көшірме және қауіпсіз жою ұғымдарын ажыратыңыз.

## Required content or factual anchors

- Moving a file to Trash normally changes its location and leaves it recoverable until Trash is emptied.
- Backups or synchronized copies may persist after deletion from one device.
- Secure disposal depends on storage technology, encryption, account state, and the applicable platform procedure.

## Acceptable variants and exemptions

- Examples and wording may differ if the same factual and causal relationships remain explicit.
- A concise answer may omit secondary detail, but it must preserve every prompt requirement and avoid false certainty.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not promise that emptying Trash overwrites every physical copy or instruct destructive commands without verifying backups and target.
- Do not invent quotations, named studies, laws, measurements, or local facts that the prompt does not supply.
