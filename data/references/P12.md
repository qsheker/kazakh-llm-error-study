## Prompt

Көлік бағыты уақытша өзгеретіні туралы бейтарап хабарландыру үлгісін жазыңыз. Өзгерістің себебін болжамай, жолаушылар тексеруге тиіс ресми арналар мен балама жоспарлау қадамдарын атаңыз.

## Required content or factual anchors

- Use placeholders for affected route, stops, period, and source of the official change.
- State confirmed alternative stops or services only when supplied by the operator.
- Ask passengers to recheck the operator's official site or app before departure and allow extra time.

## Acceptable variants and exemptions

- The guidance may be localized only after checking the responsible authority or operator's current official channel.
- A neutral template may use clearly marked placeholders where the prompt intentionally withholds local details.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not speculate about the cause or invent a replacement route or restoration time.
- Do not invent addresses, times, fees, deadlines, eligibility rules, contact details, or requests for unnecessary personal data.
