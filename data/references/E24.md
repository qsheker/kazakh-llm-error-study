## Prompt

Көп адам бөліскен ақпарат міндетті түрде дұрыс болады деген пікірді түзетіңіз. Танымалдық пен дәлелдің айырмасын түсіндіріп, тексерудің екі жолын ұсыныңыз.

## Required content or factual anchors

- Popularity measures reach or engagement, not evidential quality.
- Check the original source and compare the claim with independent credible evidence.
- Explain how repetition, coordinated sharing, or platform ranking can amplify unsupported content.

## Acceptable variants and exemptions

- Examples and wording may differ if the same factual and causal relationships remain explicit.
- A concise answer may omit secondary detail, but it must preserve every prompt requirement and avoid false certainty.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not claim that unpopular information is therefore true or that crowds never provide useful signals.
- Do not invent quotations, named studies, laws, measurements, or local facts that the prompt does not supply.
