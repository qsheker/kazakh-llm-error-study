## Required content or factual anchors

- A sample proportion varies randomly from trial to trial.
- With independent repeated trials, a larger sample usually makes the observed proportion more stable around the underlying probability.
- The coin example must compare proportions rather than guarantee an exact 50 percent outcome.

## Acceptable variants and exemptions

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not invent quotations, named studies, laws, measurements, or local facts that the prompt does not supply.
