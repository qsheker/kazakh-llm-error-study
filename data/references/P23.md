## Prompt

Суды пайдалануға уақытша шектеу туралы хабар естіген тұрғынға ақпаратты қалай тексеру керегін түсіндіріңіз. Ресми дереккөзді, қолданылу аумағын және мерзімін нақтылау қадамдарын көрсетіңіз.

## Required content or factual anchors

- Find the issuing water utility, health authority, or local government notice and verify its authenticity.
- Confirm the exact affected area, start time, expected duration, permitted uses, and update channel.
- Distinguish a conservation restriction from a boil-water or do-not-use notice and follow the exact official wording.

## Acceptable variants and exemptions

- The guidance may be localized only after checking the responsible authority or operator's current official channel.
- A neutral template may use clearly marked placeholders where the prompt intentionally withholds local details.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not merge different notice types or invent health instructions and restoration times.
- Do not invent addresses, times, fees, deadlines, eligibility rules, contact details, or requests for unnecessary personal data.
