## Prompt

Электрондық мемлекеттік қызмет порталына алғаш кіретін адамға жалпы нұсқаулық жазыңыз. Ресми доменді тексеру, қауіпсіз кіру және жұмысты аяқтаған соң аккаунттан шығу жолдарын түсіндіріңіз.

## Required content or factual anchors

- Reach the service from a known official government domain or saved official application, not an unsolicited link.
- Use a private device or trusted connection where possible, protect credentials and one-time codes, and inspect the requested permissions.
- Sign out, close the session, save the official receipt, and remove downloaded personal files from shared devices.

## Acceptable variants and exemptions

- The guidance may be localized only after checking the responsible authority or operator's current official channel.
- A neutral template may use clearly marked placeholders where the prompt intentionally withholds local details.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not ask the user to share passwords, electronic-signature keys, or one-time codes with a helper.
- Do not invent addresses, times, fees, deadlines, eligibility rules, contact details, or requests for unnecessary personal data.
