## Prompt

Интернеттегі мәлімдеменің сенімділігін тексеру жолын кезең-кезеңімен түсіндіріңіз. Алғашқы дереккөзді табу, жарияланған күнді тексеру және тәуелсіз растауды іздеу қадамдарын қосыңыз.

## Required content or factual anchors

- Trace the claim to its earliest available or primary source.
- Check author, publisher, date, context, and whether an image or quotation is reused misleadingly.
- Seek independent confirmation from sources that do not merely copy one another.

## Acceptable variants and exemptions

- Examples and wording may differ if the same factual and causal relationships remain explicit.
- A concise answer may omit secondary detail, but it must preserve every prompt requirement and avoid false certainty.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not use search-result rank, a verification badge, or share count as proof by itself.
- Do not invent quotations, named studies, laws, measurements, or local facts that the prompt does not supply.
