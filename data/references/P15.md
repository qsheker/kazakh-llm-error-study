## Prompt

Ортақ оқу кеңістігінен алған жабдықтың зақымданғанын байқаған адамға әрекет тәртібін түсіндіріңіз. Қолдануды тоқтату, қызметкерге жағдайды дәл сипаттау және өздігінен жөндемеу туралы айтыңыз.

## Required content or factual anchors

- Stop using the equipment and prevent further risk without dismantling it.
- Tell staff what was observed, when, and under what normal use, distinguishing observation from speculation.
- Follow the venue's incident and replacement process and retain any reference number.

## Acceptable variants and exemptions

- The guidance may be localized only after checking the responsible authority or operator's current official channel.
- A neutral template may use clearly marked placeholders where the prompt intentionally withholds local details.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not attempt repair, hide damage, assign blame without evidence, or keep using unsafe equipment.
- Do not invent addresses, times, fees, deadlines, eligibility rules, contact details, or requests for unnecessary personal data.

## Sources

- US National Archives: Start Your Research. https://www.archives.gov/research/start Accessed 2026-11-15. Supports: preparing a research topic and using archival catalogues and reference services.
