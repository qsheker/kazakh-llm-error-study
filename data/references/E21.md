## Prompt

Жаңалық хабары мен жарнамалық мәтінді мақсаты, тілі және дәлел қолдануы бойынша салыстырыңыз. Оқырман әрқайсысын бағалағанда нені тексеруі керегін көрсетіңіз.

## Required content or factual anchors

- A news report primarily informs about an event; advertising primarily seeks a response such as purchase or support.
- Compare source disclosure, tone, evidence, omitted conditions, and calls to action.
- Readers should verify claims and distinguish independent evidence from testimonials or sponsored content.

## Acceptable variants and exemptions

- Examples and wording may differ if the same factual and causal relationships remain explicit.
- A concise answer may omit secondary detail, but it must preserve every prompt requirement and avoid false certainty.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not assume every news item is neutral or every advertisement is factually false.
- Do not invent quotations, named studies, laws, measurements, or local facts that the prompt does not supply.
