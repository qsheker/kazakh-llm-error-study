## Prompt

Өнеркәсіптену қалалардың өсуіне қалай әсер етуі мүмкін екенін себеп-салдар тізбегімен түсіндіріңіз. Жұмыс, көші-қон және инфрақұрылым арасындағы байланысты көрсетіңіз.

## Required content or factual anchors

- Industrial employment can attract migrants and increase urban population.
- Population growth can increase demand for housing, transport, water, sanitation, and public services.
- The chain must allow variation: industrialization can reshape existing cities or create new centers, with unequal effects.

## Acceptable variants and exemptions

- Examples and wording may differ if the same factual and causal relationships remain explicit.
- A concise answer may omit secondary detail, but it must preserve every prompt requirement and avoid false certainty.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not claim every industrial project produces identical urban growth or only positive outcomes.
- Do not invent quotations, named studies, laws, measurements, or local facts that the prompt does not supply.
