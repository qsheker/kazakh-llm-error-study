## Prompt

Тақырыптағы эмоциялық сөздер оқырманның ақпаратты қабылдауына қалай әсер ететінін түсіндіріңіз. Бейтарап және эмоциялық екі қысқа тақырыпты салыстырыңыз.

## Required content or factual anchors

- Emotionally loaded wording can frame interpretation before the underlying facts are evaluated.
- The neutral and emotional headlines should refer to the same event while differing in evaluative language.
- Readers should inspect evidence, source, and context rather than infer truth from tone.

## Acceptable variants and exemptions

- Examples and wording may differ if the same factual and causal relationships remain explicit.
- A concise answer may omit secondary detail, but it must preserve every prompt requirement and avoid false certainty.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not claim emotional language always makes a statement false or that neutral wording guarantees accuracy.
- Do not invent quotations, named studies, laws, measurements, or local facts that the prompt does not supply.
