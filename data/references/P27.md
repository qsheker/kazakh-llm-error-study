## Prompt

Сатып алу түбіртегінде қате байқаған тұтынушыға мәселені шешу тәртібін түсіндіріңіз. Құжатты сақтау, сатушыға нақты жүгіну және ресми шағым арнасын тексеру қадамдарын көрсетіңіз.

## Required content or factual anchors

- Keep the original receipt and make a copy or photo that clearly shows the disputed line.
- Contact the seller through its official channel with the transaction details, specific error, and requested correction.
- Record dates and responses, then check the applicable official consumer-complaint route and payment-dispute terms if unresolved.

## Acceptable variants and exemptions

- The guidance may be localized only after checking the responsible authority or operator's current official channel.
- A neutral template may use clearly marked placeholders where the prompt intentionally withholds local details.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not alter the receipt, threaten staff, or publish payment and identity details.
- Do not invent addresses, times, fees, deadlines, eligibility rules, contact details, or requests for unnecessary personal data.

## Sources

- US FTC: Solving Problems With a Business. https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/solving-problems-business-returns-refunds-and-other-resolutions Accessed 2026-11-15. Supports: preserving records, contacting the seller, and checking complaint routes and deadlines.
