## Prompt

Арифметикалық орта мен медиана ұғымдарын түсіндіріңіз. Шағын сандық мысал құрып, деректерде өте үлкен бір мән болғанда олардың неге әртүрлі нәтиже беретінін көрсетіңіз.

## Required content or factual anchors

- The arithmetic mean is the sum divided by the number of observations.
- The median is the central ordered value, or the average of the two central values.
- A very large value pulls the mean more strongly than the median; the example must show both calculations.

## Acceptable variants and exemptions

- Examples and wording may differ if the same factual and causal relationships remain explicit.
- A concise answer may omit secondary detail, but it must preserve every prompt requirement and avoid false certainty.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not compute a median before sorting or imply that one measure is always preferable.
- Do not invent quotations, named studies, laws, measurements, or local facts that the prompt does not supply.
