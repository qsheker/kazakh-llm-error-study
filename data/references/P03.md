## Prompt

Көшедегі жарық шамы істемей тұрғанын байқаған тұрғынға мәселені хабарлау тәртібін түсіндіріңіз. Орынды сипаттау, қауіпсіз қашықтық сақтау және өтініш нөмірін жазып алу жолдарын көрсетіңіз.

## Required content or factual anchors

- Keep away from exposed wires, damaged poles, traffic, and other immediate hazards.
- Report the exact or approximate location, landmark, asset identifier, and observed condition through the responsible official channel.
- Record the request number and use it for follow-up; call emergency services only for an immediate danger.

## Acceptable variants and exemptions

- The guidance may be localized only after checking the responsible authority or operator's current official channel.
- A neutral template may use clearly marked placeholders where the prompt intentionally withholds local details.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not touch the equipment, attempt repair, or publish personal details unnecessarily.
- Do not invent addresses, times, fees, deadlines, eligibility rules, contact details, or requests for unnecessary personal data.
