## Prompt

Тарихта кез келген оқиғаның бір ғана себебі болады деген пікірді түзетіңіз. Ұзақ мерзімді жағдайлар, тікелей түрткі және адамдардың шешімдері қалай қатар әсер ететінін түсіндіріңіз.

## Required content or factual anchors

- Historical explanations can combine long-term conditions, an immediate trigger, and human choices.
- Causes operate at different levels and may interact rather than form one simple chain.
- The response should distinguish contributing conditions from a trigger and avoid claiming inevitability.

## Acceptable variants and exemptions

- Examples and wording may differ if the same factual and causal relationships remain explicit.
- A concise answer may omit secondary detail, but it must preserve every prompt requirement and avoid false certainty.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not reduce a complex event to one person, one date, or one cause without evidence.
- Do not invent quotations, named studies, laws, measurements, or local facts that the prompt does not supply.
