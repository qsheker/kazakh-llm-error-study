## Prompt

Ұшақпен сапарға дайындалған адамға багаж және арнайы көмек талаптарын қалай тексеру керегін түсіндіріңіз. Нақты шектеулерді ойдан қоспай, билет шарты мен тасымалдаушының ресми ақпаратын салыстыруды ұсыныңыз.

## Required content or factual anchors

- Compare the ticket fare conditions with the operating carrier's current baggage page.
- Verify dimensions, weight, prohibited items, mobility aids, and special assistance for the exact itinerary and connections.
- Save the carrier's confirmation and recheck close to departure.

## Acceptable variants and exemptions

- The guidance may be localized only after checking the responsible authority or operator's current official channel.
- A neutral template may use clearly marked placeholders where the prompt intentionally withholds local details.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not invent baggage allowances, fees, medical-document rules, or universal notice periods.
- Do not invent addresses, times, fees, deadlines, eligibility rules, contact details, or requests for unnecessary personal data.

## Sources

- US Department of Transportation: Traveling with a Disability. https://www.transportation.gov/individuals/aviation-consumer-protection/traveling-disability Accessed 2026-11-15. Supports: checking carrier assistance and accessibility arrangements before travel.
