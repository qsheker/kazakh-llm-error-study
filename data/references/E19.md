## Prompt

Мәтіндегі тезис пен дәлел ұғымдарын түсіндіріңіз. Қарапайым тақырып бойынша бір тезис және оны қолдайтын екі түрлі дәлел құрастырыңыз.

## Required content or factual anchors

- A thesis is a claim that the text asks the audience to accept.
- Evidence is relevant material used to support or test that claim.
- The example must include one contestable thesis and two genuinely different supporting items or reasons.

## Acceptable variants and exemptions

- Examples and wording may differ if the same factual and causal relationships remain explicit.
- A concise answer may omit secondary detail, but it must preserve every prompt requirement and avoid false certainty.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not present repetition of the thesis or popularity as evidence.
- Do not invent quotations, named studies, laws, measurements, or local facts that the prompt does not supply.
