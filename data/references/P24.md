## Prompt

Уақытша қайта өңдеу пункті ашылатыны туралы бейтарап хабарландыру үлгісін жазыңыз. Қабылданатын материалдар мен жұмыс тәртібін ойдан қоспай, тұрғындар ресми хабардан нені тексеруі керегін атаңыз.

## Required content or factual anchors

- Use placeholders for organizer, site, dates, hours, accepted materials, quantity limits, packaging, eligibility, and fees.
- Direct residents to the official notice for changes, hazardous exclusions, accessibility, and contact details.
- State only verified collection and transport instructions.

## Acceptable variants and exemptions

- The guidance may be localized only after checking the responsible authority or operator's current official channel.
- A neutral template may use clearly marked placeholders where the prompt intentionally withholds local details.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not invent accepted materials, hours, address, incentives, or disposal guarantees.
- Do not invent addresses, times, fees, deadlines, eligibility rules, contact details, or requests for unnecessary personal data.
