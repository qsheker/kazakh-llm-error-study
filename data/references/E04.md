## Prompt

Тау жыныстарының үгілуі мен топырақтың түзілуін кезең-кезеңімен түсіндіріңіз. Су, температура және тірі ағзалардың рөлін ретімен көрсетіңіз.

## Required content or factual anchors

- Weathering breaks or chemically changes rock at or near the surface.
- Water, temperature cycles, and organisms can contribute through physical and chemical processes.
- Soil develops over time from mineral material plus organic matter, water, air, and biological activity.

## Acceptable variants and exemptions

- Examples and wording may differ if the same factual and causal relationships remain explicit.
- A concise answer may omit secondary detail, but it must preserve every prompt requirement and avoid false certainty.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not present soil as instantly produced from rock or reduce all weathering to wind erosion.
- Do not invent quotations, named studies, laws, measurements, or local facts that the prompt does not supply.
