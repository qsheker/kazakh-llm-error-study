## Prompt

Тарихи кезеңдеу деген не екенін түсіндіріңіз. Оның зерттеуді реттеуге қалай көмектесетінін және кезең шекаралары неге шартты болуы мүмкін екенін көрсетіңіз.

## Required content or factual anchors

- Periodization divides a long historical sequence into analytical intervals.
- It helps organize change and continuity, but boundaries depend on the question, region, and evidence.
- Different historians may use different defensible boundaries for the same broad era.

## Acceptable variants and exemptions

- Examples and wording may differ if the same factual and causal relationships remain explicit.
- A concise answer may omit secondary detail, but it must preserve every prompt requirement and avoid false certainty.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

- Do not present period labels as natural universal facts or assume change happens everywhere on one date.
- Do not invent quotations, named studies, laws, measurements, or local facts that the prompt does not supply.
