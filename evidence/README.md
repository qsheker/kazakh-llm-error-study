# Human and institutional evidence

This directory is intentionally empty except for this guide. Do not manufacture
or self-issue the records below. Add only the real file received or completed by
the named human/institution, preserving the original file and date.

Expected release-gate files:

- `three_person_team_approval.pdf` — written instructor approval for Aldiyar,
  Yerkebulan and Iliyas to defend as one three-person team despite the generic
  two-person rule.
- `ethics_determination.pdf` — the instructor or institution's determination of
  whether the model-output/author-annotation study needs review or qualifies for
  an exemption. Do not describe an exemption before this document exists.
- `similarity_report.pdf` — the actual university similarity-system report for
  the final result-bearing manuscript.
- `reference_verification.json` — one dated record per author confirming which
  cited sources they personally read and checked. It must cover every reference,
  not just state a general promise.
- `all_author_approval.json` — dated approval of the exact final manuscript and
  author list by all three authors, including its SHA-256.
- `reproduction.json` — identity/role of a person outside the team, clean
  environment details, commands run, input commit/tag, produced hashes, and the
  outcome of reproducing every Results table and figure.

PDFs may be redacted only for unrelated personal data. Do not remove the issuer,
date, decision, covered project, or verification information needed to establish
authenticity.
